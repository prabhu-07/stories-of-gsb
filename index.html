History & Migration

The Saraswat
Lineage

Originating along the sacred Sarasvati river, Gowd Saraswat Brahmins migrated to Goa (Gomantak) and Coastal Karnataka, establishing iconic temples, mathas, and an enduring legacy of devotion spanning over five centuries.
