How to Use Google Drive Images & Folders

1. Share Settings in Google Drive: Make sure your image or folder has permissions set to "Anyone with the link can view".
2. Single Temple Photo Link: Copy link from Google Drive and paste directly into the First : Temple Image or Seventh : Images field. The system automatically converts it!
3. Entire Google Drive Folder: Paste folder link into 📁 Google Drive Folder Link to provide visitors with a one-click button to browse the complete high-resolution photo archive.
